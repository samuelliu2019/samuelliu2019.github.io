I am a 1st Year PhD Student in the [Division of Computational and Data Sciences](https://datasciences.wustl.edu/) at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://washu.edu).

My research interests lie at the intersection of data science, machine learning, and mental health. I’m interested in developing predictive models of psychological behavior that are not only accurate, but also explainable and interpretable. This includes designing robust, domain-aligned features and evaluation metrics that faithfully capture underlying constructs, while enabling effective communication of model behavior across disciplinary boundaries.

I am jointly advised by [Joshua Jackson](https://psych.wustl.edu/people/joshua-jackson) and [William Yeoh](https://engineering.washu.edu/faculty/William-Yeoh.html).

Prior to graduate school, I earned a B.A. in Statistics from [Williams College](https://math.williams.edu/) and worked in healthcare analytics at a clinical research organization.
